CC BY 4.0 allows copying, redistribution and adaptation, including commercial use. An agency can adapt a playbook for its clients, translate the connection guide, use an illustration in a presentation or include prompts in training materials.

When you share licensed material or an adaptation:

- Credit ShareOfAsk and retain supplied copyright, license and warranty-disclaimer notices.
- Link to the original material where reasonably practicable and provide the CC BY 4.0 license text or a link to it.
- Indicate your changes and retain any previous modification notices.
- Do not imply that ShareOfAsk endorses you or your work.

Credit can appear in a reasonable place for the format, such as a slide credit, report footer or linked acknowledgments page. For an adapted guide, you can use:
